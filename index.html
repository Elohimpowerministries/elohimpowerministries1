Welcome to ELOHIM POWER MINISTRIES INT'L Where Prayer Ignites Power ,Purpose and Divine Presence
ELOHIM POWER MINISTRIES INT'L is a Christ-centred ,Holy Spirit-driven prayer ministry committed to rasining believers who walk in the power of God ,fulfil their divine purpose,and become instruments of transformation in their generation.
Our mandate is to proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ with power,restore lives through fervent prayer,sound biblical teaching ,prophetic insight,and the demonstration of the Holy Spirit.

To raise a generation of prayer warriors who manifest the power of God,transform destines,and impact nations for the kingdom of Christ.
